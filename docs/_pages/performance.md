# Sleep Staging Accuracy


Technical Validation for 4-Stage Sleep Classification showed promising findings.
Results are preliminary at this time. Shown below are likely capture of
sleep spindles:

<figure style="text-align: center; display: block; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
  <img src="{{ site.url }}/OSSMM/media/performance/spindles.png" style="width: 80%; display: block; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;">
</figure>
<br>

Four sleep "spindles" are highlighted in the spectrogram, with corresponding 
arrows to the location in the time-series plot below. These phenomena correspond
to canonical spindles in sleep literature, appearing as brief bursts of activity
(approximately 0.5–2 seconds) and centered around 12–13 Hz.


The project is now aspiring to investigate 5-Stage Sleep Classification Accuracy
against Gold-standard PSG.
